* Modes
** resize
*** mode for resizing windows using h j k l
+ Pressing h will shrink the window’s width.
+ Pressing l will grow the window’s width.
+ Pressing k will shrink the window’s height.
+ Pressing j will grow the window’s height.

#+BEGIN_SRC conf
# resize window (you can also use the mouse for that)
mode "resize" {
        # These bindings trigger as soon as you enter the resize mode

        bindsym h resize shrink width 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym j resize grow height 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym k resize shrink height 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym l resize grow width 10 px or 10 ppt

        # same bindings, but for the arrow keys
        bindsym Left resize shrink width 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym Down resize grow height 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym Up resize shrink height 10 px or 10 ppt
        bindsym Right resize grow width 10 px or 10 ppt

        # back to normal: Enter or Escape or $mod+r
        bindsym Return mode "default"
        bindsym Escape mode "default"
        bindsym $mod+r mode "default"
}
#+END_SRC

** Music
*** mode for chaning track and changing volume using hjkl
+ j to decrease volume by 5
+ k to increase volume by 5
+ h to go to the previous track
+ l to go to the next track

#+BEGIN_SRC conf
mode "music" {
    bindsym j exec mpc volume -5
    bindsym k exec mpc volume +5
    bindsym l exec mpc next
    bindsym h exec mpc prev
    bindsym Return mode "default"
    bindsym Escape mode "default"
    bindsym mod1+m mode "default"
}
bindsym $mod+r mode "resize"
bindsym mod1+m mode "music"
#+END_SRC
